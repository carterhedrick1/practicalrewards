Practical Recommendation

Southwest is a different airline than it was: bags cost money now, boarding is assigned, and open seating is gone. That actually made this card more useful, because the free first checked bag for you and up to eight people on your reservation is now a real fee you're dodging rather than a perk everyone got anyway. You also get a 10% off flight code each year (excludes Basic fares), 3,000 anniversary points, boarding no later than Group 5, and the ability to grab a Standard seat within 48 hours of departure when one is open.

If you fly Southwest even once or twice a year with a checked bag, the bag savings plus the 10% code cover the $99. If you rarely touch Southwest, skip it. Bottom line: easy keeper for casual Southwest flyers who check a bag and want benefits without fuss.

Perks

  • Earning (full detail) – 2x on Southwest purchases (flights, inflight, Southwest gift cards); 2x at gas stations & grocery stores on the first $5,000 combined per year; 2x on Lyft rides through 12/31/2027; 1x on everything else. The old 2x on transit, streaming, and internet/cable/phone ended Dec 31, 2025.
  • First checked bag free (you + up to 8 companions) – Now that Southwest charges for checked bags, this is the card's headline benefit. Add your Rapid Rewards number to the reservation; a single round trip for two can cover most of the fee.
  • Group 5 boarding – You and up to eight on the same reservation board no later than Group 5 (earlier overhead-bin access).
  • Standard seat selection within 48 hours – With assigned seating now in place, cardmembers and up to eight passengers on the same reservation can pick a complimentary Standard seat within 48 hours of departure, when available — including on Basic fares, where non-cardmembers just get whatever they're assigned at check-in.
  • Anniversary & status help – 3,000 anniversary points each year; 10,000 Companion Pass qualifying points boost annually; plus 1,500 tier-qualifying points per $5,000 in card spend toward A-List.
  • 10% flight promo code (each anniversary year) – Single code deposited to your Rapid Rewards account; excludes Basic fares.
  • 25% back inflight – Automatic 25% statement credit on eligible inflight purchases when you pay with the card.
  • No foreign transaction fees – Use abroad without extra percentage fees.

Protections

  • Lost Luggage Reimbursement – Up to $3,000 per covered traveler if the carrier loses or damages your bags.
  • Baggage Delay Insurance – Up to $100/day for 3 days for essentials if your bag is delayed more than 6 hours.
  • Purchase Protection – Covers eligible new items for 120 days against damage/theft, up to $500 per item.
  • Extended Warranty – Adds 1 year on eligible U.S. warranties of 3 years or less (max 4 years total).
