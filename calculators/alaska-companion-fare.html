The catch is what you compare it with.

Suppose the eligible ticket costs $500 and the companion pays $99 plus $23 in taxes. Together, that is $622. Compared with two $500 tickets, you save $378. But if another flight you would actually take costs $700 for two, you save only $78. Count a $95 annual fee against that trip and you are $17 behind.

The annual fee changes the card-value calculation. It does not change the cash you pay at checkout if you already hold the card. Other card benefits can have value too; this tool does not guess what they are worth to you.

Which companion benefit is this?
