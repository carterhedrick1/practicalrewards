A premium card can look cheap on paper and still cost too much.

The Capital One Venture X math reaches an effective fee of −$5 when the $300 Capital One Travel credit and 10,000 anniversary miles both replace spending you would make anyway. That is the useful calculation—not the pile of benefits on the marketing page.

The catch is that the travel credit requires portal spending, and the miles are worth $100 here only at the 1¢ per mile statement-redemption value against eligible travel purchases. If either benefit changes your habits or goes unused, give it a haircut or enter $0.

Subtract only credits that replace normal spending. If the remaining fee still feels heavy, the card is too expensive for you.
