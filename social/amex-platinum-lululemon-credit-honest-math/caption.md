A coupon is not the same as cash.

The Amex Platinum offers up to $75 per calendar quarter at Lululemon. Use every credit on merchandise you already planned to buy, and the full $300 can reduce the sting of the $895 annual fee—but $595 still needs a reason to exist.

The catch is the quarterly split, required enrollment, and terms that exclude outlet purchases and gift cards. If Amex creates shopping trips that were never in the budget, the credit is spending dressed as savings.

Count only the Lululemon spend already in your budget. Keep the Platinum only if the remaining benefits justify the fee balance without manufactured spending.
