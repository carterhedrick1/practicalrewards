The headline value is real only for a fairly specific DoorDash habit.

Starting October 1, 2026, the three monthly order benefits total $35: a flexible $15 credit and two $10 discounts limited to non-restaurant orders. Across 12 months, that is $420 in face value. DashPass is separate and counts only when it saves money on orders already planned.

The catch is obvious once the perk is stripped of card-speak. Restaurant-only users can count up to $15 a month, not the full $35. Anyone placing extra grocery or retail orders just to use expiring discounts is buying a monthly chore.

Count purchases and savings already in the budget. If DoorDash is not already routine, value the order benefits at zero and ignore the coupon book.
