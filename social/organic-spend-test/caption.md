Using a credit is not the same as saving money.

A credit offsets an annual fee only when it replaces spending that was already going to happen. Record what you would normally spend—not the advertised maximum—and reduce the value for merchant switching, extra spending, or unwanted purchases.

That matters when a Chase Sapphire Reserve costs $795, an American Express Platinum costs $895, or an American Express Gold Card costs $325. Printed credit totals look tidy; real life usually needs an audit.

If you would not buy it without the card, it is not savings. Count it at $0.
